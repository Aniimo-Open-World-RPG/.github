## What Is Aniimo?

Aniimo is an open-world creature-collecting adventure developed by Pawprint Studio. Set in the fantasy world of Idyll, the game combines exploration, creature discovery, team building, battles, quests, and resource gathering.

Players take on the role of a Pathfinder and discover Aniimo, unusual creatures that inhabit the world. Each encounter offers opportunities to learn about different species, build bonds with companions, and expand a growing collection.

The adventure combines travel through varied environments with combat challenges and activities that encourage players to strengthen their teams. Discovering new creatures and learning how to use their abilities are central parts of the experience.

Aniimo supports play across several platforms, with shared progress available when players use the same account and server. This allows compatible devices to continue the same adventure.

## Key Gameplay Features

### Open-World Exploration

Explore Idyll and discover distinct landscapes, hidden locations, resources, and creatures. Travelling through the world helps players uncover new activities and expand their understanding of the environment.

### Creature Discovery and Collection

Aniimo are the central companions of the adventure. Players encounter different species, learn their characteristics, and build a collection suited to their preferred activities.

The Aniilog serves as a reference for discovered creatures and collection progress. Finding unfamiliar Aniimo adds another goal to exploration.

### Bonding with Aniimo

The relationship between a Pathfinder and their Aniimo is an important part of the game's identity. Players can develop their collection and choose companions that suit different encounters and challenges.

### Battles and Team Building

Battles involve selecting suitable companions and learning how their abilities work together. A balanced team can help players handle different opponents and encounter types.

The game includes PvE activities alongside competitive and event-based challenges. Available modes and their requirements depend on the current game content.

## World Activities and Progression

### Quests and Challenges

Quests guide players through the world and introduce new objectives. Completing activities can reveal additional areas, provide useful rewards, and support overall adventure progress.

### Resource Gathering

Resources gathered during exploration support various game activities. Collecting materials and completing objectives helps players prepare for new challenges and maintain their adventure supplies.

### Aniimo Development

Building a capable team involves discovering creatures, understanding their strengths, and preparing suitable companions for different encounters. Continued exploration creates opportunities to expand the collection and refine team choices.

### Holo-Battle Activities

Holo-Battle activities provide additional combat challenges. Certain modes feature special encounters and team-based mechanics, offering variety beyond ordinary exploration.

## Multiplayer and Shared Progress

Aniimo supports activities that bring players together through selected game modes and events. Players can participate in compatible challenges, compare teams, and take part in activities that involve other Pathfinders.

The game is available across PC, consoles, and mobile devices. Progress is shared across supported platforms when the same account is used on the same server. Accounts assigned to different servers maintain separate progress.

## System Compatibility and Performance

Aniimo is available on Windows PC, consoles, and mobile devices. The PC client is distributed through supported storefronts and the official game client.

For PC installation, the official pre-launch guidance recommends at least 40 GB of storage and an SSD. Requirements may change as compatibility is updated, so check the current official specifications before installing.

Performance depends on hardware, graphics settings, background applications, network stability, and the complexity of the current area. A stable connection is important for account access and connected game activities.

For cross-platform progress, use the same eligible account and server on each supported device. Progress on different servers is independent.

## Installation / Setup

1. Confirm that your device meets the current requirements for Aniimo.
2. Choose a supported platform and obtain the game client through its official distribution channel.
3. On PC, install the game client and allow the installation process to finish.
4. Launch Aniimo and sign in or create an account as required.
5. Select the appropriate server and create your Pathfinder character.
6. Complete the opening objectives and begin discovering Aniimo.
7. Explore the surrounding area, collect resources, and learn the basics of team building.
8. For supported cross-platform progress, sign in with the same account and use the same server on each device.
